Where you'll go

From the big city to the countryside, Yui has planned every stop.

Tokyo

Cross the famous Shibuya scramble, visit Senso-ji temple in Asakusa and eat your way through the food stalls.

Mount Fuji

Climb the steps to the Chureito pagoda for the classic view, then relax by Lake Kawaguchiko.

Kyoto

See the Golden Pavilion, hike through the torii gates of Fushimi Inari and stroll the Arashiyama bamboo grove.

Nara & Osaka

Bow to the friendly deer of Nara, then finish with street food along Osaka's Dotonbori canal.

Your 7-day itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Tokyo

    Check in to your hotel in Shinjuku and see the city lights from the free observation deck.

  2. Day 2

    Old and new Tokyo

    Morning at Senso-ji temple in Asakusa, afternoon in Harajuku and the Shibuya crossing.

  3. Day 3

    Mount Fuji day trip

    Take the bus to Fujiyoshida for the Chureito pagoda view and lunch by Lake Kawaguchiko.

  4. Day 4

    Bullet train to Kyoto

    Ride the shinkansen west, then walk the torii gates at Fushimi Inari in the late afternoon.

  5. Day 5

    Temples and bamboo

    Visit the Golden Pavilion, then explore the Arashiyama bamboo grove and the Gion district.

  6. Day 6

    Day trip to Nara

    Meet the deer in Nara Park and see the giant Buddha inside Todai-ji temple.

  7. Day 7

    Osaka food tour

    Try takoyaki and okonomiyaki in Dotonbori before flying home from Kansai airport.
